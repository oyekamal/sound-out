Sound Out · Privacy policy

App: Sound Out: Read English (package com.oyekamal.soundout) · Developer: Muhammad Kamal, Pakistan · Effective 10 October 2026

The short version

Sound Out works completely offline. It has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything a learner does stays on the phone it was done on. The developer never sees it.

What the app stores, and where

DataWhyWhere it lives
A learner profile: an automatic label such as "Child 1" or "Grown-up 1", and the track chosen (child or grown-up)To keep progress separate when more than one person uses a phoneOn the device only (the app's private browser storage, IndexedDB)
Lesson progress, check results, review cards for sounds and words, days practised, stickersTo pick the next lesson, bring back what was missed, and show progressOn the device only
Two small on-device flags: whether the first-time demo has been shown, and the app's active profileSo the demo is not repeatedOn the device only

The app never asks for a real name, an email address, an age or a date of birth. Profile labels are made by the app. Words typed into a writing exercise are used on screen to compare with a model answer and are not saved.

What the app does not do

  • No internet connection is needed or used. Lessons, pictures and every voice clip are inside the app. The Android app does not request the internet permission, and it has no code that sends or fetches anything over a network.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party software that collects data.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, files or notifications. Sound Out does not record or listen to anyone; answers are given by tapping.
  • No accounts and no in-app purchases.
  • No export, sharing or backup feature. Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this app, so progress is not copied to Google's servers or to a new phone. Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything.

Children

Sound Out is made for children from about age 4 or 5 who are learning to read, and for the older learners and adults who teach or join them. Because the app collects and transmits no personal information, the developer receives no information about any child. There is nothing to request, correct or delete on a server; deleting the app deletes the data. The app has no link that leaves the app and nothing for a child to buy.

Audio

The teacher's voice, and every letter, sound and word you hear, are pre-recorded audio files shipped inside the app. The voice is AI-generated. It was made before the app was built. Nothing you type or do in the app is sent anywhere, and no speech is generated online.

Google Play and the web version

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may keep its own records of the install under Google's privacy policy. The developer does not receive them from the app. A web version of the same course runs at oyekamal.github.io/sound-out. It is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log the technical details of a page request under its own privacy statement. In the web version the learner's data also stays in that browser only. The Android app does not load anything from the web.
