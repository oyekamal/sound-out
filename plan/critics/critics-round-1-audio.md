# Critic round 1 - Audio (plan-v1 §4 and every audio touchpoint)

Evidence: 31 af_heart clips and 8 Sara clips measured (ffmpeg ebur128, numpy), one blind Gemini A/B run (read-only key). I did not listen myself.

## Verdict: B is more likely

The plan never specifies the join between the human phoneme voice and the Kokoro word voice, and it puts the one-voice claim in its own summary ("one voice") while shipping two different voices on the very first lesson (§3 demo: human /s/ /æ/ /t/ then Kokoro "sat").

## Single biggest gap

The blending contract (Meet it, Blend ladder, every s-a-t demo) is a human-speaker to Kokoro handoff. Nothing in §4 makes the two timbres, spectral bandwidth, loudness or pace match, and nothing tests it. A child cannot blend phonemes in voice 1 into a word in voice 2 if she hears two speakers; a parent hears it in the first sitting. The plan's own consistency rule 3 only equalises loudness.

## Defects

**1. Human /s/ /æ/ /t/ vs Kokoro "sat" (§4.4 rule 3, §4.6, §3 demo).** Evidence: Kokoro af_heart is 24 kHz, energy above 8 kHz is 1.2-4.4% (ship 95% rolloff 4.0 kHz); the human kit is 48 kHz. Fricatives (/s/ /ʃ/ /θ/) carry their identity above 4 kHz, so a human /s/ will sound brighter and louder than the /s/ inside Kokoro "sat". No timbre, pitch-range or speaking-rate match is specified; "female speaker sits closest" is a hope. Fix: add a mandatory voice-match gate in week 1: speaker must be auditioned against af_heart in a 3-step s-a-t -> sat A/B, judged by 5 naive parents (not Kamal) as "same person?", plus matched F0 median within 2 semitones and a 12 kHz low-pass on the human clips so bandwidth matches. Verify: A/B test result in spikes/voice-match.md; spectral centroid of human /s/ vs Kokoro /s/ in "sat" within 15%.

**2. Loudness spec is wrong and the bakeoff proves it (§4.2 step 4, Q1).** Plan: "peak-normalise to -3 dBFS, then loudness normalise to one target ±1 LU", no target value, no standard. Measured on the bakeoff "norm" folder (peak -3.1 dBFS on every clip): integrated loudness of af_heart words ranges -17.7 to -20.8 LUFS (3.1 LU spread, cake -17.7 vs ship -20.8), phonemes -21.0 to -25.3. The Opus files in /opus are at -23.2 to -23.7. The Sara bar is -17.6 to -18.7 LUFS (seven clips, all within 1.1 LU, true peaks -1.8 to -5.1).  BS.1770 gating on sub-400 ms clips is unreliable. Fix: spec it: two-pass loudnorm to -18 LUFS integrated for words/sentences, true peak -1.5 dBTP, and for clips under 0.4 s normalise by active-segment RMS (-20 dBFS) instead of LUFS, applied to human and Kokoro clips by the same function. Q1 should measure after Opus encode/decode, not on the wav. Verify: histogram over 200 clips, sd under 0.5 LU, human vs Kokoro mean within 1 LU.

**3. Silence trim spec is ambiguous and conflicts with the reference (§4.2 step 4, Q1 "silence < 150 ms").** Raw Kokoro clips carry 290-435 ms of leading silence and 400-550 ms trailing (word_sat lead 377/trail 445); phoneme clips have only 100-240 ms of sound inside 0.5-0.7 s. "Under 150 ms" does not say whether to leave 150 ms or cut to it, and tight trimming of stops clips the burst/closure. Fix: pad to exactly 40 ms head / 80 ms tail with 5 ms fades, using an energy-threshold detector at -45 dBFS, not ffmpeg silenceremove defaults; apply to human clips too. Verify: Q1 checks lead in [30,60] ms, tail in [60,110] ms, on the decoded file.

**4. Pseudoword IPA and review load (§4.2 step 2, §4.3 Q3/Q4).** "IPA from the lexicon's p array, deterministic by construction" means the GPC table is the single point of failure, and the generator output is never validated against what Kokoro's tokenizer actually accepts. In the bakeoff, IPA input produced vop heard as "vob", chote as "Chode", blim naturalness 1/5 (Gemini). The plan has Kamal (a non-native listener, Urdu first language,) hear ~1,360 pseudowords in Q4 with the "native-speaker reviewer if one is found" as optional. Fix: make a General American native reviewer mandatory and budgeted (about 4 hours); Q3 for pseudowords must be forced-choice among 4 minimal-pair foils, not open "which word is this"; add a round-trip check that each IPA string only contains Kokoro vocabulary symbols (fail build otherwise). Verify: reviewer sign-off per pack; pseudoword error rate under 2% on a frozen 200.

**5. Re-roll policy mixes voices and breaks determinism claims (§4.4 rule 1, §4.5).** Rule 1 says one speed per tier; re-roll step 2 allows 0.9 for one clip; step 3 renders a carrier phrase "the word sat." and crops, which imports sentence prosody (falling intonation, coarticulated onset from "word") into a citation-form clip. Step 4 silently swaps a clip to the human speaker, so a word in the middle of a list is in a different voice - the exact B failure. Determinism: the clip id hashes voice + model sha + text + ipa, but not torch, misaki or espeak version, thread count or CPU; float differences across machines can shift duration and prosody, so a rebuild on another laptop changes "unchanged" clips. Fix: forbid per-clip speed; change the carrier approach to render the word as a one-word utterance with IPA stress marks only; human fallback allowed only for isolated phonemes, never words (words fall back to IPA override, then are cut from the lexicon and replaced). Pin torch/misaki/kokoro versions in the manifest and verify bit-identical sha of the wav on a second run on a second machine. Verify: render 100 clips twice, compare sha256 of the PCM.

**6. Speed 0.8/0.9 control will pitch-shift (§3 line 252 vs §7 perf line 629).** Plan: "playback rate with pitch preserved ... needs no second render"; perf plan: bundles pre-decoded into AudioContext. AudioBufferSourceNode.playbackRate does not preserve pitch (only HTMLMediaElement.preservesPitch does), so 0.8 sounds like a lower, slower, different speaker, and decoded bundles at 48 kHz are the path that breaks it. Fix: either render a slow variant for L1-L2 only (about 2.2k clips, 5 MB) or use a time-stretch worklet; decide before bundling.  Verify: F0 median at 0.8 within 3% of 1.0.

**7. Prosody for sentences and QA blindness (§4.3, §4.4 rule 2).** Q2 Whisper-check and Q3 Gemini both transcribe words and ignore prosody; sentences are "naturalness 4/5" in the bakeoff except the child sentence "The cat sat on the mat." scored 2/5 (unexplained, still unheard). The Q4 random 200 per pack is a binary "clear and correct", not a consistency rating. Fix: add a rubric to Q4 (same speaker as the neighbours? pace? sentence intonation OK?) and make the first Kamal listen on the 100 L1 clips include all 50 Track A sentences before any more build work. Verify: a parent listener, not the author, scores 20 clips for "one teacher?" at 4/5 or more.

## Not a defect (measured)

Blind Gemini A/B, 5 Kokoro words vs 5 Sara Urdu clips (different language, voice-only prompt): Kokoro won 5/5 on "warm human teacher" (human-like 5 vs Sara 1-3, Sara described "muffled"). Sara clips are 22.05 kHz mono mp3 at about 32-37 kbps, -17.9 LUFS. So Kokoro at word level is not worse than the bar; the failure is the human/TTS join, loudness spec and phonemes. Weak evidence: one run, LLM judge, languages differ. Also: listen_judge.py is Urdu-only; the "English prompts" do not exist yet.
