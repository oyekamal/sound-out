# Critics round 5: parent and adult (plan-v5)

Sources: plan-v5.md, both bar JSONs, Common Sense (ABC: kids cannot skip ahead, mic needed; Khan: email account, glitches). Every number below is the plan's own budget or estimate, none measured.

## Verdicts after one week

**1. Rawalpindi mother (kids 5 and 8): B stays.** Khan Kids and ABC give both children a character, a book or a game in the first minute. Sound Out's week is 3 to 5 letters, a village piece and an English story the 5-year-old cannot follow, so she will not keep a third app on a 3 GB phone.

**2. Recife father (6-year-old): A stays, narrowly.** It is the only one of the three that speaks Portuguese to him (Khan has Spanish only), and 40 MB beats 200+ MB on 2 GB. That holds only if the 2.5 MB pack actually lands on mobile data (defect 1).

**3. Shopkeeper (38, 15 min a night): B stays.** Main Duolingo gives him a word and a streak in two minutes. Sound Out budgets 18 minutes for night one, three over his 15, and sends him home with nothing if one mini check slips.

## The single biggest stop

**Night one, shopkeeper, the first missed mini check (§3.6, §3.3).** At minute 8 he has heard /s/ and /a/, seen no word, and the app says "Tomorrow you will read your first word." The plan states the adult bar twice with different numbers: §3.3 default 4/5, §3.6 "each sound behind a >=90% mini check". On a 5-item check, 90% means 5/5. A learner who is right 85% of the time passes one such check 44% of the time and three in a row 8.5% of the time. He also has to hear made-up words ("tas / sas / tat") in a second language with no meaning to hold them. The fallback (print concepts + A + B, "sat" on night two) triggers only if more than 20 minutes for 3 of 5 testers, in week 3. He has already quit Duolingo once.

## Defects (6)

**1. §3.1 step 3 and §4.1 pack prompts: the language question and the mobile-data wall.**
- Failing moment: the father sees "Português (Brasil)" first (device locale) and taps it, as anyone would. The plan's own fixture has Lívia "skip". Packs default to Wi-Fi-only, and a mobile-data download needs the grown-up gate plus a prompt of "MB, Wi-Fi recommended, minutes", which is English text. A spoken English line about "Levels 3-4 about 20-32 MB" also precedes the first sound. The Rawalpindi mother has no Wi-Fi at all, so Urdu help may never arrive.
- Better apps: one download, nothing else asked; Khan's Spanish read-to-me ships inside the app.
- Verify: first launch on data only, choose Portuguese, time it to the first Portuguese line.?

**2. §3.8 Listen & Talk and §3.5 Stories: English comprehension for a child with no English.**
- Failing moment: Ayesha is asked a spoken English question and taps one of 3 pictures (33% by chance). The Urdu gloss plays after the questions, by design, so it cannot help. Tier-2 words are defined in English. The pilot bar is ">=80% with and without a pack", but no no-English child has been tested.
- Better apps: Khan Kids "Read To Me" and ABC stories are wordless-friendly: tap anything and it reacts, with no right-answer pressure.
- Verify: 5 non-English children, no pack, one Stories session; log pictures tapped per question and whether any quit.

**3. §3.1 doors, picture lock, grown-up gate: the 8-year-old defeats all three.**
- Failing moment: the gate is "a written sum". Hamza, 8, solves it, so he can skip lessons, change the lock, add or delete a profile and start downloads. The picture lock (2 of 4 pictures, 12 orderings) he guesses in at most 3 tries, 30 s lockout, retry. The plan admits the lock is not security, yet it is the only wall between siblings. Ayesha, 5, must recall two pictures in order after every 10 idle minutes.
- Better apps: Khan uses profile tiles with no lock. ABC has no locks.
- Verify: hand a phone to an 8-year-old and time how long he takes to pass the sum and open a sibling's profile.

**4. §3.9 grown-up view: the mother cannot do "Tonight, ask them these 3 sounds".**
- Failing moment: she does not know English sounds, so she cannot tell /s/ from /sh/ when her child says them. The view is "English text + icons". "Listen to her read" appears only if a helper exists, and none exists in her house. The "Show your grown-up" handover therefore lands on a parent who can neither read nor judge it.
- Better apps: Khan's parent screen shows finished activities and a visible progress bar; ABC shows stories completed. Both are glanceable without English.
- Verify: 3 non-English-reading parents, "what do you do tonight", the plan's own test, with a stopwatch on time to a confident answer.

**5. §3.6 adult night one: 18 minutes, PIN first, child-coded entry.**
- Failing moment: the first thing the shopkeeper meets is "Who is reading?" with Pebble pointing, then a forced 4-digit PIN ("Later" once), then 3 minutes of print concepts before any sound. Track B hides the village and stickers, but the entry screen, the animal-picture tiles on the doors and the Pebble pointer are shared with Track A. At minute 15 he sits on a 12 s window per item (§3.4; 3 timeouts end the check) reading Latin letters he met 7 minutes ago.
- Better apps: Duolingo sets a goal in two taps and shows a word in two minutes.
- Verify: screenshot every adult screen from icon to first word and count child-coded assets; stopwatch 5 shopkeepers with 15-minute cutoffs, not 20.

**6. §3.3 sittings on demand for the 5-year-old, alone.**
- Failing moment: "One more?" appears only after a passed mini check, and the plan expects about 366 sittings for L1.02 to L4, roughly 12 months at one a day. Week one is /s/, /a/, /t/, a sticker per sitting and a village piece. She shows her mother a sticker and an English-only grown-up view. Her mother gets no "words read" count; that is Track B only.
- Better apps: Khan rewards her with a book or activity she can name.
- Verify: log what the 5-year-old does in days 2 to 7 without an adult, how many sittings run and how many end at a failed mini check, and what the mother says when asked "what did she learn".

## Still unproven

- **90 s for the Recife child** (G0): a budget, not a measurement. "Which starts with /s/? sun / map / fan" uses "starts with", a concept Pebble cannot gesture once. A lucky tap (33%) stops the clock.
- **Install size**: ~37-53 MB beats Khan 201 MiB and ABC 212 MiB, but L3-L4 (20-32 MB) and L5 (12-18 MB) hit defect 1's wall.
- **English-reading adult required?** For night one, no. For the Wi-Fi/gate prompts, the grown-up view, "Listen to her read" and "mastered", yes, or an English helper the plan assumes.
