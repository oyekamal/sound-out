## 2. Hard requirements (PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL, one line of evidence each)

**Kapi (capybara)**
- Distinct from Duo/Khan/TYM/markhor: PASS. No capybara in any bar shot; not a goat.
- Works for a 40-year-old: PARTIAL. Calm face and scarf are adult-neutral, but the round head, dot-level detail and tiny arms read as a children's sticker.
- Global, culture-neutral: PASS. Animal, no text, neutral scarf.
- Readable at 48 px: PASS. icon_48 reads as a capybara head.
- Identity across 4 poses: PASS. Head, scarf and body are the same across sheet.png.
- Acts like a teacher: PARTIAL. Point (pose 3) and listen (hand to ear, pose 2) read. No correct/"not quite" pose.

**Tola (tortoise)**
- Distinct: PARTIAL. A green turtle sits in the Teach Your Monster shot (bar/teach_monster_2.jpg). The idea overlaps; the art does not.
- Adult-friendly: PASS. Closed smile, calm eyes.
- Culture-neutral: PASS. Patience is a shared fable, not a culture marker.
- 48 px: PASS. Head and shell read.
- Identity across poses: PASS with drift. The sheet's green is more saturated than the hero's olive.
- Acts like a teacher: PARTIAL. Point and listen read; no correction pose.

**Noo (cloud)**
- Distinct: PASS.
- Adult-friendly: FAIL. Blush cheeks, big catchlit eyes and a small smile read as a toddler's sticker.
- Culture-neutral: PASS.
- 48 px: PASS, but the face is the only detail that survives.
- Identity across poses: FAIL. Silhouette changes: pose 3 is flatter and narrower than pose 1, and an extra bump appears in pose 2.
- Acts like a teacher: FAIL. The point is a stub tendril; no clear listen gesture; no correction.

**Axi (axolotl)**
- Distinct: PASS.
- Adult-friendly: FAIL. Same blush-and-big-eyes baby cues in all four poses.
- Culture-neutral: PARTIAL. Strong Mexican cultural association (Xochimilco, Xolotl).
- 48 px: PASS. Pink silhouette reads; frills mush.
- Identity across poses: PASS. Body and frills are stable.
- Acts like a teacher: PARTIAL. Point (pose 3) reads; the "listen" raised hand is not an ear gesture; no correction.

**Lumo (lantern)**
- Distinct: PASS.
- Adult-friendly: PASS. Neutral mood.
- Culture-neutral: FAIL. A lit lantern is a specific festival object (Ramadan fanoos in Pakistan and the Gulf, Diwali, Lunar New Year). The key calls it "an object, so culture-neutral"; I disagree.
- 48 px: FAIL. icon_48 reads as a handbag or purse.
- Identity across poses: PARTIAL. Glow and body stable; arm placement changes a lot between poses.
- Acts like a teacher: PARTIAL. Point reads; listen does not; no correction.

## 3. Biggest remaining gap in the top concept (Kapi)

It cannot visibly correct. The face has a mouth change but no brow or eye change, and three of four poses share the same frontal stance. There is no "not quite, try again" expression that stays warm. The bar teaches by reaction, and Kapi has one reaction it can show.

## 4. Ranking

1. Kapi: passes distinctness, culture, 48 px and identity; partial on age and teaching.
2. Tola: closest to Kapi, but borderline on distinctness against the TYM turtle.
3. Axi: babyish in every pose.
4. Noo: babyish, unstable silhouette, weak pointing.
5. Lumo: fails 48 px (reads as a bag) and culture-neutrality.

## 5. Bar verdict

None of the five beats or matches the Duolingo ABC cast. In my blind read the cast won 5/5. Kapi is the best starting point. The next round needs a real expression range (brows, eyes, a correction pose), a less babyish face, and a 48 px silhouette test before any concept is called ready.
