## 2. Copy check

- **Art: FAIL (generic-stock risk).** Tilo is a flat-vector capybara with a teal-and-cream rope strap, a tan pouch and a teal earring. That is the same look as many stock and clip-art capybaras. The rounded cream-muzzle head in the 48 px icon also reads like the Animal Crossing villager style. I cannot name a specific copyrighted character it copies, but the bucket is crowded and it is not yet ownable.
- **Name "Tilo": PASS with caveats.** Two open syllables (TEE-lo), easy in every target language. Spanish *tilo* is the linden tree, which is warm. Near-neighbours to watch: "Tito" (a picture-book capybara), "Taptilo" (a braille app) and German "Thilo". Web search is not a trademark search; run USPTO/WIPO/EUIPO and app-store checks before launch.

## 3. Hard requirements (PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL, one line of evidence each)

- **Warm, not deadpan or meme:** PASS. Most poses and faces are a closed smile or open joy; no meme-face.
- **Not babyish for a 40-year-old:** PARTIAL. The celebrate pose with sparkles and arms up leans kid-app; the rest is adult-neutral.
- **Try-again reads as encouraging:** FAIL. The try pose is an open palm presenting; there is no clear warm "not quite, try again" face or pose. Same gap as round 2.
- **Model-a-sound reads as speaking, mmm/aaa/ooo distinguishable:** PARTIAL. mmm (closed line) and aaa (open mouth) are distinct. ooo is a small brown ring on the muzzle that reads as a snout blob, not a round mouth, and aaa reads closer to a shout or surprise than a vowel.
- **Listen vs think distinct:** PASS in full poses (hand cupped to ear vs finger to chin). PARTIAL on faces: both are eyes-up at face-crop size.
- **Silhouettes distinguishable:** PASS. The IoU table tops out at 0.90 (model vs point); the strip shows six distinct shapes.
- **Identity identical across poses and faces:** PARTIAL. Ear, strap, earring and pouch hold. The muzzle length and head shape drift between the body sheet and the face sheet.
- **Icon readable at 48 px:** PARTIAL. It reads as a small brown animal with a cream muzzle and an eye. Capybara only shows clearly at 96 px and up.
- **Culture-neutral:** PASS. An animal, no text, no festival or dress cues.

## 4. Biggest remaining gap, and bar verdict

The biggest gap is **expression range that a learner reads instantly, plus a cast.** Duolingo's characters change face and body together, and the bar has several distinct characters. Tilo is one figure with subtle face changes, a presenting-not-encouraging try pose, and a mouth-shape set where ooo and aaa do not read clearly as sounds. Fix the try-again pose and face first, then redraw the mouth shapes at small size, then add at least one more cast member before the voice-teacher claim.

**Bar verdict: Tilo does not yet match or beat the Duolingo ABC cast.** It is clearly ahead of Kapi (round 2: 1/3), and it wins against a no-character or story-splash Duolingo screen. It loses or ties against the cast screen, and the art still reads as generic stock.
