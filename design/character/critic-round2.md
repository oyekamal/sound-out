## 2. Copy check

- **Name collision, FAIL until cleared.** "Kapi" is one step from "Kapibara-san", an existing, widely known capybara character. Species, name and a mascot role all overlap. A trademark and character search is needed before this name ships.

That is the same bucket as many clip-art capybaras, which is not distinctive enough for a bar that wants a named cast.

- **Try-again face reads as warm concern:** FAIL. The retry face is a furrowed brow over half-lidded eyes with a tight, flat mouth. It reads as skeptical or unimpressed, not warm.
- **Not babyish for a 40-year-old:** PASS (marginal). No blush, no oversized eyes. The sparkle-wave celebrate pose and the sticker-flat style lean kid-app.
- **Culture-neutral:** PASS. An animal, no text, no festival or dress cues.
- **Readable at 48 px:** FAIL. The icon shows a brown head with a cream muzzle. The face and expression are gone, and the species is unclear.
- **Can visibly teach:**
  - Point: PASS. Clear arm and finger toward the object in p1.
  - Listen (hand cupped to ear): PASS.
  - Model a sound: FAIL. The "model" pose is a wide-open mouth with a hand at the chin. It reads as a yawn or gasp, not a clear mouth shape a learner can copy.
  - Correct / try again: FAIL. No correct or try-again pose appears in the sheet. The correct pose (open palm) reads as presenting, not confirming.

## 4. Biggest remaining gap, and bar verdict

The biggest gap is **reaction range that carries emotion at a glance.** Duolingo's mascots change the whole face and body (wide eyes, open mouth, arms up) so the learner knows the result before reading. Kapi changes one or two facial features and holds a passive body, so its reactions are subtle and in places misleading: retry reads as judgement, model reads as a yawn. A teacher that cannot show "yes, that's it" or "not quite, try again" warmly cannot yet teach the way the bar does. Fix this before anything else, and resolve the Kapibara-san name collision in the same pass.

**Bar verdict: Kapi does not match or beat the Duolingo ABC cast.** It improved over round 1, where the cast won 5/5 and Kapi was the best of five. Round 2 is 1/3 in Kapi's favour, with a key error and a name collision still open. Not ready to be the voice teacher.
