# Character name, round 3

"Kapi" fails (critic round 2): one step from "Kapibara-san", an existing capybara character, and close to "capy" / "capybara".

## Rules used
- 1 or 2 syllables; only easy sounds for Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, Urdu and Swahili speakers (no p/v/r/th/sh, no clusters; open vowels a e i o u; consonants m n l t k d).
- No rude or odd meaning in a major language.
- Not close to capybara / kapibara / capy, nor to a known mascot or brand.

## Six candidates, with search evidence (WebSearch, 2026-10-09)

| Name | Search result | Meaning check | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tilo** | "Tilo" mascot / app: no mascot found; only a toddler sound-game UI kit on Gumroad ("Tilo games app for kid") and Taptilo, a braille device app (different word). "Tilo" capybara: nothing (nearest capybara character is "Tito" in a picture book, *The Cheeky Capybara*). "Tilo" + learn-to-read app: nothing. | Spanish: *tilo* = linden tree (tila tea is a calming drink). German first name (variant of Thilo). Rap-dictionary slang entry for sneakers is unverified. Also checked and dropped before the six: **Nilo** (the learning app *Lili & Nilo* for ages 2-6, Niloya the Turkish cartoon), **Tomi/Tomo** (Tomo is the YOMU reading-app character), **Domi** (Domi Domi toddler apps), **Melo/Mello** (Death Note character, Mello Yello), **Mimo** (coding app).

## Choice: Tilo

- Two open syllables, ti-lo, every sound exists in all six target languages.
- No mascot, learning app or capybara found under that name; the nearest hits (a UI kit, a braille device, a picture-book capybara called Tito) are far enough away that it would not be confused with them.
- The one real meaning is a warm one: Spanish *tilo* is the linden tree, whose flowers make a calming tea. That fits a patient teacher.
- Not near "capybara", "capy" or "kapibara".

Caveats: web search is not a trademark search. Before a store launch, run a USPTO/WIPO/EUIPO check for class 9 and 41 and a Play/App Store name search. "Taptilo" and German first-name use of "Tilo" are the nearest neighbours to watch. Backups: Moku, Tolu.

Folders: `design/character/tilo/` is the new home. `design/character/kapi/` stays untouched as history.
