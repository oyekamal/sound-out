# Name and ASO — Sound Out

**Brand:** Sound Out · **Play title (23/30 chars):** `Sound Out: Read English` · **Package:** `com.oyekamal.soundout` · **Repo:** oyekamal/sound-out

## Why this name
- "Sound it out" is the one instruction every phonics teacher gives; it names the method, works for a 5-year-old and a 38-year-old, and is not child-coded (the plan's Track B rule).
- Short enough to leave room for the top keyword phrase in the title; "English" is the word non-native adults everywhere search with, so it sits in the title, not the description.
- **Global first (Kamal, 2026-10-06):** the listing is written for the whole world. No language or country appears in the title, short description or first screenshot. Home-language help is mentioned once, as "help in your language (optional)".
- Play search on 2026-10-05 found no app titled "Sound Out" for reading (nearest: "Speak Out Kids", "Letter Sounds A to Z"). Trademark search NOT done — Kamal to check before upload.

## Long description — opening lines (draft)
Sound Out teaches anyone to read English from the first letter sound to real books, one small sitting a day. Built on synthetic phonics, the method with the strongest evidence, and on a free open course. Works fully offline. Children get a playful path with a mascot and stickers; teens and adults get the same lessons in a grown-up skin with everyday texts. No ads, no account, nothing leaves your phone.
